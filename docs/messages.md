# Everything the shop says to people

This is every message the shop sends, written out as it actually arrives.

**How to use it:** cross things out, rewrite them, scribble in the margin —
whatever is easiest. Hand it back and the site gets changed to match.

**About the highlighted bits:** anything in **bold** changes with each order.
The examples below use a made-up customer so you can see the shape of it:

> **Ali Hassan**, ali.hassan@example.com, 07700 900321
> Order **ASB-4F7K** — al-Nahw al-Wadih ×2 (£12.00) and Riyad al-Salihin (£12.50)
> Subtotal £24.50 · postage £3.95 · **total £28.45**

Where a message changes depending on the order — posting it or collecting it,
paying now or paying cash on the day — every version is shown.

**Two channels.** Email always goes. Telegram only goes to customers who tapped
"Connect Telegram" on their order. When both go out, the same news is sent twice
in slightly different words — those pairs are marked so you can keep them
saying the same thing.

# Part one — Emails

Every email arrives with the shop's name and mark at the top, and
`assubkibooks.co.uk` at the bottom. **If a customer hits reply, it comes to
you** — not to a no-reply address.

## 3. You confirm the order → to the customer

Sent when you press "Confirm and send". **This one also goes by Telegram** — see
Part two, section B. If neither can be delivered, the order goes back in your
queue and nothing is sent, so an order can never look handled when the customer
was never told.

The payment wording comes from your saved drafts in Settings — **three for
posted orders, two for collections**. When you confirm an order you pick which
one to use by its name, and you can still edit it on the order before it goes. A
draft you have left empty is not offered.

### If they are paying now

**Subject:** Order **ASB-4F7K** confirmed - **£28.45** to pay
**Big heading:** Your order is confirmed
**Underneath it:** Reference **ASB-4F7K** · **£28.45** to pay

> السلام عليكم **Ali**,
>
> Your books are reserved. Here is the total and how to pay.

> al-Nahw al-Wadih ×2 — £12.00
> Riyad al-Salihin — £12.50
> Subtotal £24.50
> Postage £3.95 — only shown on posted orders
> **Total to pay £28.45**

Then a grey box:

> **How to pay**
>
> **Please send payment by bank transfer, quoting your order reference so we can
> match it. Account name: As-Subkī Books. Sort code: 00-00-00. Account number:
> 00000000.**
>
> Please quote **ASB-4F7K** with your payment.

Then `[ View your order ]` and:

> Once your payment reaches us we will confirm and post your books.

### If they are collecting and paying cash on the day

Same email, five things worded differently:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | Order **ASB-4F7K** confirmed - **£24.50** on collection |
| **Opening line** | Your books are set aside. Here is what they come to - you can pay in cash when you collect. |
| **The total row** | Total, payable on collection |
| **Grey box heading** | Collecting your books |
| **Under the box** | Quote **ASB-4F7K** when you collect. |
| **Last line** | No payment is taken on our website. Message us to agree a time and the books will be waiting. |

Ticking "paying in cash on collection" does not pick the wording for you — it
asks. A note appears telling you to check the wording suits somebody paying at
the door, and you choose whichever of your two collection drafts fits.

✅ **Changed:** there used to be a separate, fixed cash wording. It was a third
draft to maintain for a case the picker already covers, so it is gone — its
wording became your second collection draft.

## 8. The 48 hours run out

**Nothing is sent at all.** The hold quietly lapses and the books go back on the
shelf. If the customer happens to open their link again, the page says:

> **Hold lapsed** — We did not hear back within 48 hours, so the books returned
> to the shelf. Nothing was charged - you are welcome to request again.

*(Settled: no warning before it lapses. It stays silent, as it is now.)*

# Part three — What their order page says

Not sent anywhere, but the emails above are written to match it. If you change
the wording of a message, this should usually change too — otherwise the email
and the page tell them slightly different things.

| Where the order is | The heading | Underneath it |
|---|---|---|
| Just placed | Request received | Your books are held. We will reply with the total and how to pay. |
| You have sent payment details | Awaiting payment | We have sent you payment details. Your books stay held until payment reaches us. |
| Confirmed, cash on collection | Ready to arrange collection | Your books are being set aside at **12 Evington Road…**. Get in touch and we will agree a time - you can pay in cash when you collect. |
| Ready, already paid | Ready to collect | Thank you. Your books are set aside for you at **12 Evington Road…**. |
| Ready, paying cash | Ready to collect | Your books are ready at **12 Evington Road…**. Contact us to arrange a collection time. Payment can be made when you collect. |
| Paid, being posted | Payment received | Thank you. Your order is being packed. |
| Posted | Posted | Your books are on their way. |
| Delivered | Delivered | Your order is complete. جزاكم الله خيرا. |
| Collected | Collected | Your order is complete. جزاكم الله خيرا. |
| Cancelled | Cancelled | This request was cancelled. Nothing was charged. |
| Hold lapsed | Hold lapsed | We did not hear back within 48 hours, so the books returned to the shelf. Nothing was charged - you are welcome to request again. |

**The progress steps down the page:**

- **Request received** — Your books were set aside for you.
- **Confirmed, payment details sent** — while they wait: *We are checking the
  books and working out the total.* On a cash collection this step is called
  **Confirmed, collection to arrange** instead.
- **Ready to collect** — before: *We will set them aside and agree a time with
  you* (cash) or *Once your payment reaches us your books will be set aside*
  (already paying). After: *Come and collect - you can pay when you arrive*
  (cash) or *Come and collect whenever suits you*.
- **Collected** — We will mark this when you pick them up.
- **Payment received** *(posted orders)* — Once your payment reaches us we will
  confirm here.
- **Posted** — We will post your books and note it here.
- **Delivered** — We will close this off once it reaches you.
